A Little Letter, Just for You

Hi [her name],

I'm so grateful you came into my life. I didn't plan for you to matter this much, but you do.

I notice the little things you do: how you listen, how you laugh, how you make a plain day feel lighter. I don't think I thank you for them enough.

I'd love to keep making memories with you, and I hope we stay in each other's lives for a long time.

I also want you to know that your feelings matter to me. Whatever happens, I want you to be happy, and I'll respect whatever you choose.

No matter how many words I write, I don't think I'll ever fully explain how much I appreciate having you in my life. Thank you for being you. 💙

— [Your name]
