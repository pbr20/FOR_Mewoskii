Promises I Want to Keep

These are sincere intentions, not a claim that life will always be perfect. They are what I will try to do, every time.

  1. I'll Be There

    I promise to be there for you, not only during your happiest moments, but also when life gets difficult. I’ll always be there for you, even when life gets busy and things become difficult. No matter how much life changes, I hope you’ll always know that you can count on me.

  2. I'll Listen

    I promise to listen when you need someone to talk to, without judging you or making you feel unheard.

  3. I'll Respect You

    I promise to respect your feelings, opinions, dreams, boundaries, and individuality.

  4. I'll Keep Choosing You

    I'll always choose you for whoever you are and so I'll keep making an effort to understand you and make time for you, even when life gets busy.

  5. I'll Be Genuine

    I’ll make sure my feelings for you are always genuine and honest. I never want you to question whether what I feel for you is real but if you even feel its not genuine you can always talk to me about it.

  6. I'll Make Memories

    I want to create countless beautiful memories with you, from little everyday moments to special occasions.

I know I'll get some things wrong. What I can promise is that I'll care, I'll own my mistakes, and I'll keep trying. That's all I really have to offer, and I mean every word of it.
